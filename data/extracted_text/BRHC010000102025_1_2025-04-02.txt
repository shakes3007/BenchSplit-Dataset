ORAL ORDER
2 02-04-2025 Heard learned counsel for the petitioner, learned
counsel for the informant and learned APP for the State.
2. The petitioner has preferred this application for
grant of regular bail in connection with Fatuha P.S. Case No.
191 of 2024 dated 27.03.2024 registered for the offences
punishable under Sections 341, 323, 307, 302 read with Section
34 of the Indian Penal Code and Section 27 of the Arms Act.
3. As per the prosecution case, the petitioner and the
co-accused persons are alleged to have assaulted the informant’s
brother with lathi, danda and iron rod. It is further alleged that
the co-accused, Ranjeet Singh assaulted on the head of the
informant’s brother with an iron rod with intention to kill due to
which he got injured and fell on the ground. Thereafter, all the

4. Learned counsel for the petitioner has submitted

petitioner. The petitioner has no concern with the alleged
occurrence. The petitioner has no criminal antecedent as stated
in para 3 of the bail petition. The petitioner is in custody since
21.07.2024.
5. Learned A.P.P. for the State and learned counsel for
the informant have opposed the bail petition of the petitioner
and submitted that as per post-mortem report, the deceased had
four external injuries which amply demonstrates that the
deceased was assaulted by the accused persons.
