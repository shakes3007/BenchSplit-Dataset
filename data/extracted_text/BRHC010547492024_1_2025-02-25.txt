dealy as we find that a trend is developing now at the Bar not to
calculate the period of limitation and mention it in the
application seeking condonation of delay. This practice needs to
be discouraged.
2. We further find that even the affidavit enclosed
with the application does not confirm to the requirements of law
to state which part of the statement made in the application are
true to the knowledge of the deponent and which part are based
on the information derived from the records. It cannot said to be
